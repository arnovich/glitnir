# Glitnir 0.1.14 release notes

Changes since 0.1.13.

## Proofs in the Notebook Inspector

- Inspect a proof in the selected claim’s Inspector. The step picker and
  navigation show the tactic, its source location, and Lean’s goals and
  context before and after it. The pencil opens the exact tactic in VS Code.
- Recorded goal splits appear as inference bars. Select a linked subgoal to jump to
  its recorded tactic. Compound tactics can still appear as a single step when
  their intermediate goals are outside the recording.
- Inspect proof sits below Proof steps. Once recorded, Refresh sits beside
  the heading. Refreshing keeps the previous recording visible while Lean
  runs if it still matches the saved source and dependencies. Checking a claim remains a separate action at the bottom.
- The Inspector displays the saved check status once, with colour reflecting
  its freshness. The separate proof view and its shortcuts are retired.

## Claim graph in Explore

- View claim graph beside Contents opens Explore on the selected claim. Switch
  between its direct relationships and the full graph.
- The canvas has more room and fits the graph on entry. Fit graph restores
  the current graph’s fit after zooming, and Show in Notebook returns to the
  declaration.
- Graph details distinguish missing dependency records from empty lists.
  Notebook help stays above adjacent theorem cards.

## Distribution

The extension, backend, and optional Forseti plugin ship as 0.1.14.
The thin VSIX requires Python 3.12+ and Lean 4.35.0. The Forseti plugin keeps
its backend requirement of `>=0.1.10,<0.2`.

Verify the exact candidate assets and fresh installations on macOS and Linux
before publication.
